Problem

Building a Single Page Was Slow, Complex, & Unpredictable

Content managers had to manually assemble 8–22 blocks to create a single campaign page, navigating nearly 1,000 block types with little guidance. Finding the right components and configuring nested structures was time-consuming and error-prone, especially for new or infrequent users.

On top of that, there was no clear way to preview the final output before publishing, making the process feel uncertain and frustrating.

Every block has a block type (banner, collection, etc); 1 block = 1 component on a page.

Why was it so difficult?

😵‍💫 Overwhelming Complexity

Content managers had to identify the correct block type from nearly 1,000 available options, often without clear guidance.

⏳ Time-Consuming Setup

A single page could require configuring 8–22 individual blocks, many containing multiple nested layers.

❓ Flying Blind

Users couldn't confidently verify how content would appear until after publishing, resulting in constant back-and-forth revisions.
