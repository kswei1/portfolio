How do you fix a platform that has outgrown its own structure — without the risk of rebuilding it?当一个平台已经“长大到”超出自身结构，你如何在不承担重建风险的前提下修复它？

Recommended argument建议主线

Section 3 · Designing within constraints第 3 节 · 在约束中设计

High risk, so collaboration became part of the design高风险，于是协作成了设计的一部分

SLIDE-05A high-risk platform — improve via UI, preserve the backend高风险平台——用 UI 改进、保留后端supported
Judgment判断

Backend changes risk the live site, so a full rebuild was off the table. The design question narrowed to: solve a structural problem primarily through UI/frontend. Reads as product maturity, not an excuse.改后端会危及线上站点，因此全面重建不可行。设计问题收窄为：主要通过 UI/前端解决结构问题。这体现的是产品成熟度，而非借口。

SLIDE-06I drove the exploration and aligned PM & engineering我主导探索，并对齐 PM 与工程stated-only
Agency主导

Shown as a loop (feedback → exploration → prototype → PM/Eng feasibility → iteration → MVP/post-MVP), not a generic UX timeline. Emphasize decisions and alignment.以循环呈现（反馈 → 探索 → 原型 → PM/工程可行性 → 迭代 → MVP/后续），而非泛泛的 UX 时间线。强调决策与对齐。

Evidence证据

Research board, prototypes, and workshop captures are placeholders — currently narrative. See GAP-02.研究看板、原型与工作坊素材都是占位——目前仅叙述。见 GAP-02。

SLIDE-07Couldn't change the component — so I changed the interaction (Drawer)无法替换组件——于是我改了交互（抽屉）supported
Tradeoff取舍

The Drawer was tied to the architecture, so replacing it was unsafe. Stacking drawers meant users went 5 deep, then closed 5 times. Fix: switch the drawer's content instead of stacking, and reduce shadows. Same component, new interaction model.抽屉与架构深度耦合，替换不安全。层层堆叠让用户深入 5 层、再点 5 次关闭。解法：切换抽屉内容而非堆叠，并弱化阴影。同一组件，新的交互模型。

Why strongest为何最强

Concrete, honest, and proof of pragmatic design within technical limits. Give it a clear before/after visual.具体、诚实，且证明了在技术限制内的务实设计。给它一个清晰的前/后对比图。

SLIDE-09Why three panels — three needs, one workspace为何是三栏——三种需求，一个工作区supported
Reasoning依据

Four inputs converged: competitive patterns, user mental model, the need for capabilities + work + preview to coexist, and future extensibility (AI). Three panels were the result, not the goal.四个输入汇聚：竞品模式、用户心智模型、能力+工作+预览需共存、以及面向未来的可扩展性（AI）。三栏是结果，不是目标。

SLIDE-10Designed to scale — flexible panel configurations为扩展而设计——灵活的栏位组合inferred
Argument论点

Feature+Main, Main+Preview, AI+Main, etc. New capabilities plug in without a new layout — this is what makes it a foundation, not a one-off UI.功能+主区、主区+预览、AI+主区 等。新能力可直接接入而无需新布局——这正是它成为“底座”而非“一次性 UI”的原因。

SLIDE-11Designed for what's next — a place for AI (Blocky)为未来而设计——为 AI（Blocky）留位inferred
Job作用

Blocky as an optional panel — evidence the architecture evolves with the product, not a separate AI project. Keep it short so it doesn't take over.Blocky 作为可选栏——证明架构能随产品演进，而非另起一个 AI 项目。保持简短，别喧宾夺主。
