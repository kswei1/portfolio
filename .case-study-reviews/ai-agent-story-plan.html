Central question核心问题

As Blocky grows into the internal platform's main UI, why couldn't users tap its new power — and what finally unlocked it?当 Blocky 逐渐成为内部平台的主界面，用户为何用不上它的新能力——又是什么最终打开了这道门？

Recommended argument建议论点

Blocky was becoming the main UI but was still shaped like a chatbot — it couldn't teach, move fast, or grow. The redesign worked on two fronts: make it usable now — after even a guided wizard didn't teach new users what a good prompt is, a ready-made template did (create-a-block ~1 min → ~10 sec in early testing) — and make it a platform — an extensible structure and a brand identity for the surface it's becoming.Blocky 正在成为主界面，却仍是“聊天机器人”的样子——它不会教、不够快、也长不大。这次重设计从两条战线推进：先让它现在就好用——在连引导式向导都没能教会新用户“好提示”之后，一个现成模板做到了（早期测试中，创建 block 从约 1 分钟降到约 10 秒）；再让它成为平台——为它正在成为的主界面，建立可扩展的结构与品牌身份。

Current argument (as written)当前论点（现有文案）

"Redesigned the agent from a free-form chatbot into a guided experience." — true, but it hides the more interesting turn: guidance alone (the wizard) wasn't enough.“把自由聊天式 Agent 重新设计成引导式体验。”——没错，但它掩盖了更有意思的转折：仅有引导（向导）并不够。

Protect保留

The wizard → template reversal is your five-second turn — build the arc on it.

“向导 → 模板”的反转，就是你的五秒转折——把整条弧线建立在它之上。

The strongest beat is the discovery that even a step-by-step wizard didn't teach prompt quality. Frame it as an evolution, not a failure — you kept the wizard, modernized it, and scoped it to post-MVP, while the template became the first-use unlock. Let SLIDE-02/03 build the question; don't leak the template answer before SLIDE-04.

最有力的情节，是你发现连一步步的向导也没能教会“提示词质量”。把它讲成“演进”而非“失败”——你保留了向导、将其现代化并排入 post-MVP，而模板成为首次使用的解锁点。让 SLIDE-02/03 制造疑问；在 SLIDE-04 之前不要泄露“模板”这个答案。

Structure结构

Two acts resolve the altitude mismatch — small unlock in Act 1, big vision in Act 2.

两幕结构化解了“高度不匹配”——第一幕是小解锁，第二幕是大愿景。

The onboarding unlock (template) is a craft win but felt small against the "main UI" stake. Splitting into Act 1 (make it usable now) and Act 2 (make it a platform) gives the extensible structure (SLIDE-07) and brand identity (SLIDE-08) their own act, and lets the closing (SLIDE-09) tie both. Keep Act 2 tight so it reads as payoff, not scope-dumping.

入门解锁（模板）是手艺上的胜利，但相对“主界面”的利害显得小。拆成第一幕（让它现在就好用）与第二幕（让它成为平台），让可扩展结构（SLIDE-07）与品牌身份（SLIDE-08）各得其所，并由结尾（SLIDE-09）收束两幕。第二幕保持精简，读作“回报”而非“堆职责”。

Label如实标注

The 1 min → 10 sec number is a usability test, not production adoption.

1 分钟 → 10 秒是可用性测试结果，不是上线采用数据。

It's a strong directional result — state it as "in testing," with how it was measured. Add adoption/task-completion once it's live. Don't let it read as a shipped metric it isn't yet. See GAP-04.
